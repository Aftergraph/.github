# Code of Conduct

Aftergraph welcomes technical disagreement, skeptical review and evidence-based criticism. Those are features, not social defects.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful toward contributors, reviewers and users.
- Critique claims, code, methods and evidence rather than people.
- Give reproducible counterexamples when possible.
- Distinguish fact, inference and speculation.
- Admit uncertainty and correct mistakes when evidence changes.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries and authorization limits.
- Keep discussions relevant enough that future readers can reconstruct the decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, knowingly false allegations, spam, or attempts to pressure maintainers into hiding contradictory evidence are not acceptable.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the process in `SECURITY.md`, not weaponized in public discussion.

## Research culture

Aftergraph explicitly values failed replications, negative results, prior art and falsification attempts. A contribution that disproves a favored hypothesis can be more useful than another page claiming everything is "state of the art" because apparently adjectives now count as benchmarks.

Maintainers may moderate or remove content that violates this policy and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
